# Rep Review

A side-by-side video coach that runs in the browser. Compare a clip of yourself with a reference clip, side by side or as a ghost overlay.

**Open it:** the GitHub Pages link for this repository.

## Features

- Two clips side by side or stacked (picked automatically from their shapes), or one laid over the other as an adjustable ghost
- Link the clips with several sync points so different tempos stay matched
- Frame-by-frame stepping at each clip's own frame rate (detected automatically), slow motion (0.1×, ¼×, ½×) and section looping
- Single-clip view and a full-screen view that shows only the videos and drawings
- Freehand, line, arrow, circle and angle drawing tools
- Zoom, pan and mirror
- Snapshots of the current view, saved as images
- Record the video area and drawings with your voice, as an MP4 for review or a vertical 9:16 video for social media

## Privacy

Videos never leave your device. When you load a clip, the browser plays it straight from your phone or computer. Nothing is uploaded, including to GitHub.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Space play/pause · ← → step a frame (Shift for 10) · 1 / 2 single clip · F full screen · ? all shortcuts · L link · P add sync point · O overlay · S snapshot · R record · [ ] loop start/end · Ctrl+Z undo
